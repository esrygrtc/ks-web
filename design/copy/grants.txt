KeepState Grants: compute for the people building the agents.
The harnesses we wrap were written by people who were not paid to make our product work. Grants are how we settle that: session credits, engineering time, and a compatibility row of your own so your users can see the freeze holds.
You wrote the agent people actually use. We wrap it, gate it, and keep the wrap current at your release cadence rather than ours.
Long-horizon agent studies need sessions that outlive the machine. A trial that dies at hour 40 is not a result, it is a lost week.
No institutional affiliation required and no proposal review board. If you are learning to build agents, the meter should not be the thing that stops you.
Day-0 support is a promise: new harness releases get a wrapped image within 48 hours.
Not a logo, not a case study, not an exclusivity clause. Three things, all of which we would want anyway:
Tell us when the freeze does not hold for your harness. A failed absorption is a finding we publish, and it is worth more to us than a testimonial.
Names appear only where the grantee asked for them. The rest are described by what they build.
Four questions. A human reads it, and you get a yes or a no with a reason, never silence.
No pitch deck, no call required. If we need more we will ask one question by email.
