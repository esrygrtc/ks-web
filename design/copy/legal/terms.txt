The agreement between your organization and KeepState, Inc. for use of the durable agent session cloud. Written to be read once by a person rather than skimmed by a lawyer.
You keep ownership of everything your agents produce. We run the infrastructure, meter it honestly, and can suspend an account for non-payment or abuse with notice. Neither side promises the other unlimited liability, and either side can leave with your data intact.
A summary is not the agreement. Where the two disagree, the clauses below govern.
KeepState hosts agent sessions in isolated micro-VMs, checkpoints their state, and brokers model calls through a metering gateway. Availability commitments for each surface are stated in the service level agreement and measured by an external probe rather than by our own reporting.
We do not read your checkpoints except through a break-glass path that notifies your Owners and writes to your audit log.
You pay for session-minutes, checkpoint storage after deduplication, and, if you use our inference, a spread on brokered tokens. Meter output is authoritative for billing, and your ledgers show the same figures we bill from, line by line.
Checkpoints under a legal hold survive termination until the hold is lifted, and you will be told which ones.
Each side’s aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or a breach of confidentiality obligations, where no cap applies. Neither side is liable for indirect or consequential loss.
