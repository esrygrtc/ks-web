# Verbatim lines carried from design/Legal & Support.dc.html (Data Processing Agreement).
Where session contents are personal data, you are the controller and we are the processor. This document is the instruction set, and it is the one your reviewer will actually read.
We process session contents only to run the sessions you asked for. We use approved subprocessors, keep data in the region you pinned, notify you of a breach within 24 hours, and delete or return everything on termination.
You determine what your agents do and what data they touch, which makes you the controller. We host, checkpoint, and restore, which makes us the processor. Our instructions come from your use of the product: a checkpoint happens because you set a cadence, not because we decided to keep a copy.
Support: only when you open a ticket and only through the break-glass path, which notifies your Owners and writes to your audit log.
Technical measures are described in full in the architecture overview. The load-bearing ones: hardware-virtualized single-tenant guests, encryption at rest with per-organization keys, provider credentials held outside the isolation boundary, and credential scanning on checkpoint write.
We notify you within 24 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your session contents, with what we know at that point rather than waiting for a complete picture. Subsequent updates follow as the investigation proceeds, and the write-up is published in the findings archive once affected customers have been told.
On termination you may export everything through the API. After 30 days we delete session contents, including from backups within a further 30 days. Checkpoints under legal hold are the exception and are itemized to you rather than silently retained.
