The parts, and the laws that govern them.

Every component the console and the site are built from, rendered in each state it can hold. Three laws come before the parts, because they decide what a part is allowed to say.

UI Laws

LAW 1

Absence is not zero

Missing instrumentation reports as unavailable, never as zero. A zero is a measurement.

measured

0

The meter reported zero tokens. This is a fact.

absent

unavailable (meter unreachable)

The meter said nothing. We refuse to invent a number.

LAW 2

Every number carries provenance

Measured, or estimated. Dollars derived from external rates are estimated even when their tokens are measured.

LAW 3

A claim links its evidence

Any claim-shaped sentence may carry a diamond to the gate run that proves it. Unproven sentences soften to observation.
